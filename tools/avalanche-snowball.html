How this is calculated

Both strategies run the identical budget - all minimums plus your extra (and windfall) - month by month. The only difference is targeting: avalanche aims every spare dollar at the highest APR; snowball at the smallest balance. When a debt dies, its minimum rolls into the attack - that rolling is where both strategies get their power.

Which should you pick?

Mathematically, avalanche always wins or ties - the number above is its edge on your debts. But the gap is often smaller than people expect, and snowball's early kills are real fuel for the humans actually doing this. Honest rule: if the avalanche edge is under a few hundred dollars, pick whichever keeps you paying. If it is thousands, let math win.

Notes

Assumes fixed APRs, no new charges, constant minimums (card minimums actually shrink with the balance - paying the fixed amount is already a win). If neither strategy finishes, the payments do not cover interest and the results will say so.
