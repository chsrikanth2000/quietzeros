How this is calculated

Transport cost depends on how you move and how far: a DIY truck rental scales with distance and truck size; full-service long-distance movers price by estimated shipment weight × distance, the way real mover quotes work, not a flat fee. Local moves (100 miles or less) use a flat or hourly local rate instead. Packing, insurance, storage, temporary lodging or a home-overlap period, utility deposits, vehicle re-registration and pet relocation are added as flat or scaled estimates you can edit.

An employer relocation payment is netted out last. Since the 2017 tax law suspended the moving-expense deduction for civilians — made permanent by the 2025 tax law — any reimbursement your employer pays is ordinary taxable W-2 income unless they've specifically grossed it up to cover that tax. We assume it's taxed at your marginal rate unless you say otherwise.
