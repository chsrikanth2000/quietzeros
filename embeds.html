Ground rules

Free for any site, commercial or not. The badge links back to the source page — please leave that in place; it's the whole trade. Data is Freddie Mac's Primary Mortgage Market Survey, and the widget makes exactly one request, to our server, for the numbers — no cookies, no tracking of your visitors, ever.
